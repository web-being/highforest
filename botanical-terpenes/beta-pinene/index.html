β-Pinene

C10H16 Monoterpene

What are terpenes? Boiling points Do terpenes do anything?

The second pinene. The same atoms and the same two fused rings as α-⁠pinene, with the double bond outside the ring instead of inside it.

Scent
green, woody, resinous, pine-like Reference description: “dry, woody or resinous aroma” (HSDB)
Found in
fir, spruce and pine needles, rosemary, basil, dill and parsley, nutmeg.
In our honeys
Sunshine Soothing Berry as a flavouring ingredient; amount not published
In food
Evaluated as a flavouring in 2004 by JECFA, the FAO/WHO expert committee on food additives: no safety concern at current levels of intake when used as a flavouring agent. Its entry in the JECFA database

What has been studied So far: animals

  • AnimalsA 2007 rodent study of two eucalyptus-oil compounds found pain relief from cineole. β-⁠Pinene worked weakly, in rats only, and reversed morphine’s pain relief about as much as the blocking drug naloxone does.8
  • ReviewReviewed together with α-⁠pinene for antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory activity in cells and animals.6
  • Not knownAny effect in people. One animal study of the compound itself is cited here.
