β-Pinene

C10H16 Monoterpene

The eleven Boiling points Do terpenes do anything?

The second pinene, made of the same atoms as α-pinene. It has the same two fused rings, with the double bond outside the ring instead of inside it.

Scent
green, woody, resinous, pine-like Reference description: “dry, woody or resinous aroma” (HSDB)
Found in

In High Forest honeys β-pinene is a flavouring. High Forest has not published how much a jar contains, and nobody has tested the honeys themselves. How to read the research

What has been studied highest rung: animals

  • AnimalsA 2007 rodent study of two eucalyptus-oil compounds found pain relief from cineole. β-Pinene worked weakly, in rats only, and reversed morphine’s pain relief about as much as the blocking drug naloxone does.8
  • ReviewReviewed together with α-pinene for antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory activity in cells and animals.6
  • Not knownAny effect in people. This atlas cites one animal study of the compound itself.
